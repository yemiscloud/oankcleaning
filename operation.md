## Overview of Our Services

OANK Cleaning CIC provides professional cleaning services across Paisley, Renfrewshire and Greater Glasgow. We offer both commercial cleaning and domestic cleaning, including End of Tenancy cleaning.

What makes us different is that we are a Community Interest Company. This means we run as a social enterprise. Our commercial cleaning work generates income that helps fund two community programmes:

1. **Home Reset** – targeted cleaning support for people who struggle to keep their homes clean due to practical barriers.
2. **Employment Pathway** – paid training and work experience for people who face barriers to getting a job.

In short, when you book a cleaning service with us, you are supporting a company that reinvests surplus into community benefit rather than private profit.

## 3. Community Project: Home Reset & Employment Pathway Pilot

### Home Reset
Home Reset is our planned community project providing targeted household-cleaning support to people who experience genuine practical barriers to maintaining their homes.

A person may have accommodation and may receive support towards their housing costs, but this does not necessarily mean they have the practical ability, resources, mobility, confidence or other support required to keep their living environment clean, hygienic and manageable.

**What Home Reset does:**
- Focuses on agreed household-cleaning tasks
- Supports dignity, wellbeing and the ability to maintain a suitable living environment

**What Home Reset does NOT do:**
- Medical treatment
- Nursing
- Personal care
- Regulated social-care services

**Access:**  
People normally access Home Reset through an appropriate referral or assessment process. OANK assesses eligibility, safety and the suitability of the requested work before accepting a case.

### Employment Pathway
The Employment Pathway creates opportunities for people who face barriers to employment.

OANK:
- Recruits suitable individuals
- Provides induction and cleaning/workplace training
- Provides supervised paid cleaning experience

**Training covers:**
- Safe cleaning practices
- Use of cleaning equipment and products
- Workplace health and safety
- Professional conduct
- Communication and client boundaries
- Confidentiality
- Safeguarding awareness
- Practical workplace skills
- COSHH compliance

Workers receive supervised paid work and feedback. Progression is measured through competencies, additional work opportunities, further training or other appropriate employment pathways.

### Community Benefit & Sustainability
OANK is developing a social-enterprise model where:
- Commercial cleaning activity generates legitimate trading income
- Community-focused Home Reset activity addresses practical household barriers
- The Employment Pathway creates paid work and skills-development opportunities
- Appropriate grants, partnerships or commissioned funding help establish and expand the community programme

OANK keeps appropriate records of referrals, services delivered, hours of support, employment/training activity and outcomes so that community benefit can be demonstrated.

**Longer-term aim:**  
To develop a sustainable CIC that continues creating community benefit through a combination of trading income, partnerships, commissioned work and suitable funding, rather than relying solely on grants.

## 4. Safeguarding & Professional Boundaries Policy

Because our cleaning specialists operate inside private domestic residences, care facilities and community environments, we enforce an uncompromised safeguarding protocol:

- **Enhanced Disclosure Scotland / DBS Vetting**  
  All personnel undergo full criminal history disclosure checks and employment verification prior to client deployment.

- **Strict Client Boundaries**  
  Staff are forbidden from handling client financial matters, accepting gifts, administering medication, or giving personal care.

- **Confidentiality & Discretion**  
  All client information, personal living conditions and keyholding access codes are protected by binding confidentiality agreements.

- **Escalation & Referral Protocols**  
  Staff are trained to identify signs of abuse or neglect and follow standardised reporting to our Designated Safeguarding Lead.
