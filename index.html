OANK Cleaning CIC – Cleaning Services Across Renfrewshire and Glasgow

OANK Cleaning CIC provides cleaning at customers’ homes and workplaces across the whole of Renfrewshire and Glasgow. We are a service-area business and do not receive customers at a public office.

Our cleaning services and starting prices

  • Regular home cleaning – from £18.50 per hour.
  • Deep cleaning – from £85 one-off.
  • End-of-tenancy cleaning – from £145 per package.
  • Office and commercial cleaning – from £19.50 per hour on contract.
  • Care home and healthcare cleaning – custom quote.
  • Carpet and upholstery cleaning – from £45 per room.

Prices are starting prices. The final quote depends on the property and agreed cleaning scope.

Our community programmes

OANK Home Reset provides free or subsidised cleaning support for people facing difficult circumstances. The level of support depends on individual needs and circumstances.

Our Employment Pathway supports employment and job training opportunities connected with OANK cleaning work.
