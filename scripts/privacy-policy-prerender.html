Privacy Policy

OANK Cleaning CIC explains how it collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when visitors use the website, contact the team, request a quote or use the online chat. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

1. Who we are

OANK Cleaning CIC is the organisation responsible for the personal information described in this policy (the data controller). Contact us at info@oankcleaning.co.uk or +44 77 3195 6727. We provide cleaning services across the whole of Renfrewshire and Glasgow and do not receive customers at a public office.

2. Personal information we collect

  • Contact and enquiry details: name, email address, telephone number, service requested, postcode or general location, preferred date or days, and message.
  • Quote and booking-request details: property type, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, cleaning frequency, selected service and add-ons, preferred days or date, postcode, contact details, notes and any quote estimate.
  • Chat information: messages sent to the website receptionist and its replies, a session reference, timestamps, IP address and browser user-agent information. Chat messages are recorded for enquiry handling, service review and security.
  • Cookie and device information: saved cookie preferences and, only where you consent, information collected by Google Analytics and Meta Pixel.
  • Technical and security information generated when your browser communicates with the website, which may include IP address, browser type, timestamps and diagnostic or error information.

3. How and why we use your information

  • To respond to questions, contact you about an enquiry and provide requested quotes.
  • To assess cleaning requirements, arrange appointments and provide requested services.
  • To maintain enquiry, booking, customer-service and business records.
  • To operate, protect, troubleshoot and improve the website and chat feature.
  • To understand website usage and measure advertising through Google Analytics and Meta Pixel only if you give the relevant optional consent.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, accounting and record-keeping obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims where necessary.

4. Our lawful bases

We rely on steps before a contract and contract to handle quote or service requests; legitimate interests to respond to general enquiries, maintain appropriate records, operate and secure the website, review chat interactions and prevent misuse; legal obligation where records must be kept or disclosed by law; and consent for optional analytics and advertising technologies. You can withdraw consent at any time through Cookie settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

5. Cookies, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel

Essential browser storage remembers your cookie choice and supports core website functions. Optional technologies remain off unless you enable them. Google Analytics (measurement ID G-LQB16ZXJEC) helps us understand website use and is enabled only if you accept Analytics. Meta Pixel (ID 1801963148593783), connected to OANK Cleaning's Meta Business account, helps measure advertising and campaign performance and is enabled only if you accept Marketing. Use Accept all, Reject optional cookies or Cookie settings in the consent panel. You can later change your choice using the Cookie settings control. Rejecting optional technologies does not prevent website use.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. Where necessary, information may be shared with website hosting, database and technical providers; email and communications providers; Google when you consent to Analytics; Meta when you consent to Marketing; and professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other authorities where required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety. The website receptionist uses a response engine built into our website code; the current chat code does not send chat messages to an external generative-AI service.

7. International transfers

Some technology providers, including analytics and advertising providers, may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted international transfer takes place, we will use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards required by UK data protection law. Contact us for further information about applicable safeguards.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected. Enquiries and quote requests are kept while we handle the request and any resulting customer relationship, then only as long as needed for legitimate business, legal, accounting or claims purposes. Chat records are kept for customer-service, quality-review and security purposes and deleted when no longer needed. Cookie preferences remain in browser storage until changed or cleared. Analytics and advertising information is subject to provider retention settings and policies and your consent choices. Retention depends on the nature of the record, applicable obligations and whether a dispute or claim remains possible. We review information and delete or anonymise it when no longer required.

9. Keeping information secure

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Information sent between your browser and our website is intended to be protected using HTTPS where supported. No website or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to access your information, correct it, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests (including direct marketing where applicable), request a portable copy where the right applies, and withdraw consent. Email info@oankcleaning.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month, subject to lawful extension.

11. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a concern. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

12. Children

Our website and cleaning services are intended for people arranging services or making business enquiries, not for children to use independently. Please do not knowingly submit a child's personal information unless necessary for a legitimate enquiry and you are authorised to provide it.

13. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to services such as WhatsApp or social-media platforms. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to your use of their services. We do not control external websites.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
